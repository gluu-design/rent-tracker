How a listing gets its score

Building signals

  • Stabilized units on the building's DOF tax bill (Property Information Portal, openigloo, JustFix, Lucid Rents): +15 to +35, more the higher the share of stabilized units
  • On the DHCR / Rent Guidelines Board building list: +15
  • Built before 1974 with 6+ units, a rental and not a condo or co-op: +10
  • Active 421-a, J-51, 485-x or 467-m tax benefit: +20
  • Fewer than 6 units and built before 1974, or a condo/co-op unit: −30 to −40

Unit signals

  • Listing says stabilized, or mentions an RS lease rider / DHCR: +50 ("Declared")
  • A rent step equals a past rent × the RGB guideline, to within about $5: +25
  • Odd price that isn't a round or marketing number: +10. A ×00/×50/×95/×99 price: −5
  • Market-style swings (cuts, COVID dips, irregular jumps on renewals): −15
  • Other units in the same building declared stabilized: +10

Reading the score

  • 70+: very likely stabilized. Ask for the RS lease rider
  • 50–69: likely. Ask the broker directly
  • 30–49: possible. Worth one question
  • A big jump at vacancy doesn't rule a unit out: it can be the move from a preferential rent to the legal rent, or an IAI increase
  • Since 2019, high rent no longer deregulates an apartment
